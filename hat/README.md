# The Hat
The hat is convertible from a pointed cap to a brimmed cap. The current idea is the brim of the hat will flip down and the underside of the brim will light up once the brim is down. The brightness of the lights is controllable through buttons on the palm quire. While the controls live in the palm quire, they are independent of the palm quire's electroncs. So even if the palm quire itself is off, the hat is still controllable.

When the brim is up, it hugs the cone of the cap so it looks like it's an embellishment.

## Supplies
* 1x Arduino Nano
* 1x RGB LED 
* 2x buttons
* 1x TX480e
* 1x RX118sa
